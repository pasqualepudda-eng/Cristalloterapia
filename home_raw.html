      Why Yoga? Why Now?

      Yoga is more than a physical practice—it’s a pathway to well-being for individuals and communities alike:

      For Your Body

      Improve strength, flexibility, and overall physical health.

      For Your Mind

      Find calm, focus, and manage stress in your daily life.

      For Your Community

      Connect, grow, and uplift the yoga community.

      Learn About Yoga

      Your Partner in Yoga

      At Yoga Alliance, we believe yoga is for everybody, everywhere.

      We build community by supporting yoga teachers, schools, and advocates, expanding access, challenging outdated ideas about who yoga is for, and ensuring that the practice is inclusive and accessible to all.

      We lead with integrity, kindness, and bold action to get closer to our vision: More Yoga, Better World.

      Learn More About Yoga Alliance

      Expanding Yoga’s Reach, Together

      Yoga has the power to heal, uplift, and unite communities. But not everyone has access to its benefits.

      At Yoga Alliance, we work alongside teachers, organizations, and advocates to bring yoga to more people—wherever they are, in whatever circumstances. From refugee support programs to school-based wellness initiatives, we partner with those already making a difference to help amplify their impact.

      By supporting this work, you help ensure that yoga reaches more communities as a tool for well-being, resilience, and connection.
